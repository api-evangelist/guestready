About GuestReady:

GuestReady is a full-service short-term rental management company operating across multiple international markets, including Europe and the Middle East.

The company provides end-to-end property management services for short-term rentals (e.g. Airbnb and similar platforms), often referred to as Airbnb management or holiday home management, handling all operational aspects on behalf of property owners and investors. This includes listing optimisation, dynamic pricing, guest communication, cleaning, maintenance, and on-the-ground operations.

GuestReady also manages the distribution and monetisation of properties by listing and selling short-term stays across major online travel agencies (OTAs) such as Airbnb, Booking.com, and others, as well as through its own direct booking channels.

GuestReady’s core audience consists of:

- Individual property owners seeking a hands-off solution
- Real estate investors looking to maximise rental income
- Institutional investors requiring a professional operator

GuestReady operates as a hospitality and property management service provider, not as a software platform. Its value lies in delivering operational excellence, revenue performance, and a seamless experience for both guests and property owners.

The company is positioned as an operator within the short-term rental and hospitality space, comparable to a boutique hotel operator or professional property manager.

Relationship between GuestReady and RentalReady:

GuestReady and RentalReady are two distinct brands with different audiences, products, and value propositions.

GuestReady is a full-service short-term rental management company. It provides end-to-end property management services to property owners and investors, operating properties on their behalf.

RentalReady is a property management software designed for professional property managers and operators. It provides tools to manage operations such as guest communication, revenue management, cleaning coordination, channel management, and day-to-day workflows

Although RentalReady was originally developed by GuestReady to support its internal operations, it is now offered as a standalone solution to external companies. It is built by property managers, based on real operational experience.

It is important to clearly distinguish the two:

- GuestReady is a service provider for property owners
- RentalReady is a software solution for property managers

They do not compete with each other and should not be used interchangeably. Their audiences, use cases, and offerings are fundamentally different.

GuestReady focuses on operating properties.

RentalReady focuses on enabling others to operate properties.
